# Archive: Daisy Seed S1 prototype

This folder is the original OpenChord firmware and docs, moved here in September 2026.

It is **not** deleted and it is **not** OpenChord S1. It is a frozen prototype: Daisy Seed, 11 keys, joystick, OLED, subtractive synth, plugin/track architecture, 3D enclosure.

Keep it for reference (chord engine experiments, pin lessons, enclosure). Do not keep developing it as if it were the product line.

Active work is [OpenChord M1](../../m1/).
